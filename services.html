Available Support Services

  1. Transportation Assistance
    • Northeastern University Shuttle: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily
    • MBTA Blue Bikes (bike share): Stations at Ruggles & Museum of Fine Arts
    • Uber/Lyft discount codes available via Student Services
  2. Maintenance & Housing Support
    • Facilities Work Orders: Submit online 24/7
    • Emergency Maintenance Line: (617) 373-2222 (24/7)
    • Routine repairs: Completed within 5 business days
  3. Food & Dining Options Nearby
    • Wollaston's Market – 369 Huntington Ave (open until 10 PM)
    • Sweetgreen & Chipotle – Prudential Center (11 AM – 9 PM)
    • Huntington Avenue Eateries – Over 20 options within 0.5 mile
  4. Cleaning & Laundry
    • On-campus laundry: All residence halls (6 AM – 2 AM)
    • Professional cleaning service referrals available
    • Eco-friendly laundry pods provided monthly
  5. Mental Health Crisis Support
    • University Health and Counseling Services (UHCS): (617) 373-2772 (24/7)
    • Find@Northeastern After-Hours Support Line: (617) 373-7591
    • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
